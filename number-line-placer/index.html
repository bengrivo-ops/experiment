Number Line Placer

Whole numbers

Place numbers between the marked points, from 0–10 up to 0–1,000.

Fractions

Fractions are numbers too: each one has its own spot on the line.
Teacher settings
Why it works. How accurately children place numbers on a line predicts their maths achievement, and practice with feedback on the true position makes their mental number line more linear. For fractions, placing them on a line builds the idea that a fraction is one number with a size, which is what fraction interventions that use number lines target. Every placement shows the real spot and how far off the guess was. Placed numbers stay on the line as new reference points. There is no timer, so pupils reason instead of guessing. Sources: Siegler and colleagues on number line estimation · IES practice guide: developing effective fractions instruction
